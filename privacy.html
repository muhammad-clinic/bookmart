Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 2026

At Book Mart, accessible from our online website, we respect your privacy and are committed to protecting any personal information you share with us when placing orders or interacting with our platform.

1. Information We Collect

When you browse our website and use our WhatsApp checkout system, we may collect minimal necessary information to fulfill your order, including:

  • Your name and contact details (provided voluntarily via WhatsApp chat).
  • Delivery address and order specifications.
  • Basic technical logs handled securely by GitHub hosting platforms.

2. How We Use Your Information

The information you provide is exclusively used for:

  • Processing, confirming, and dispatching your book orders.
  • Communicating order status updates directly through our official WhatsApp contact channel.
  • Improving our website layout, user experience, and book inventory selections.

3. WhatsApp Communications

Our ordering system directs checkout summaries through WhatsApp (Number: 03244219842). Any text messages, order lists, and customer interactions transmitted via WhatsApp are governed by WhatsApp's own privacy frameworks and terms of service.

4. Data Security

We implement sensible security measures to protect your confidentiality. Because orders are completed directly via encrypted messaging channels, your sensitive financial records or credit details are never stored on our web server.

5. Contact Us

If you have any questions or concerns regarding our Privacy Policy, feel free to reach out to us directly through our official WhatsApp order line at 03244219842.
